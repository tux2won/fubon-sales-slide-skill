# Story comics

Story-comic style, derived from six square training cards. Use mint #4CD2B9, brown #44272C, white, and a pale pink background approximately #F3E9E9. The pink is a visual estimate. Use bold brown panel outlines, mint speech bubbles, and restrained peach/pink supporting colors. Prefer available YouandiModern fonts; fall back to Pretendard/Noto Sans KR. On a 1080px square, target 58–76px headings, 28–36px dialogue, and 18–22px notes. Cover: roughly 40% message and 55% two-panel scene. Body: 12–16% scene heading, 72–76% two or three comic panels, 10–12% sources and training label; use 4–5% margins and consistent 5–8px brown borders. A slight diagonal division is optional. Follow situation → misconception/problem → explanation → learning check. Limit each speech bubble to one or two sentences and make the speaker unambiguous. Keep faces, clothing, proportions, and flat illustration style consistent. Use permitted supplied artwork; do not invent an official character. Product conditions remain accurate inside dialogue. Show verified numbers as separate text or simple accurate bars with units, dates, and sources. Move complex product tables to a separate classroom page. End with a recap and learning check. Avoid overcrowded bubbles, mixed 3D/flat styles, guaranteed-return implications, fear-based claims, and missing conditions.
