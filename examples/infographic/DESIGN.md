# Infographic cards

Infographic-card style, derived from twelve square training cards. Use a white inner panel, large Korean-led headings, prominent verified numbers, a small Part label, and a consistent footer brand band. Choose one color variant for the entire deck: teal #11A1AB/#1FBCC8, or blue #0088FF with pale blue #E9F5FF. Keep dark text #282828 and restrained red #C00000 for conditions. Prefer the available YouandiModern family, with Pretendard/Noto Sans KR fallback. For a 1080px square, aim for 60–80px headings, 30–38px body, 72–100px key numbers, and readable 18–22px sources. Cover: a clear question/topic plus an optional supporting character in the lower right. Body: 20–24% heading/Part, 60–66% comparison/check/diagram, 12–16% sources and disclosures. Choose either rounded white panels or a teal window-like frame and repeat it consistently. Communicate one main point with two or three supporting elements. Use paired values, purposeful checks, horizontal bars, life-stage flows, or a layered structure; retain the conditions when simplifying tables. Every chart needs units, year, population, and comparable bases. Characters support the explanation without covering facts. Reuse permitted supplied assets consistently; otherwise use simple explanatory icons, not a counterfeit of the original character. Closing: recap or learning check. Adapt 16:9 into a message-plus-diagram composition. Avoid excessive text, multiple claims, inconsistent chart scales, alarmist wording, and unreadable notes.
